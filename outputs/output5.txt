Question 4

Explain how India's approach to strategic autonomy aligns with the broader geopolitical trends of economic resilience and international partnerships.

Answer:

India pursues strategic autonomy while expanding diplomatic engagement through partnerships with countries across Asia, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and the Americas. It focuses on economic growth, technology cooperation, and regional security. This aligns with broader geopolitical trends emphasizing economic resilience, strategic partnerships, and balancing national interests with international cooperation.
