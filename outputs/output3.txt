Question 3

How do advancements in artificial intelligence and semiconductor manufacturing influence both national security and global economic competitiveness?

Answer:

Artificial intelligence and semiconductor manufacturing are increasingly viewed as matters of national security and economic competitiveness. Governments are investing in AI research, semiconductor manufacturing, quantum computing, cybersecurity, and digital infrastructure. Additionally, strategic competition between the United States and China includes advanced semiconductor technology, artificial intelligence, and supply chain security, highlighting their geopolitical significance.
